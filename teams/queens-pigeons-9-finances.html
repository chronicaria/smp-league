How Finances Work

Revenue

  • Every win +$5M — all 36 weigh the same
  • Playoff berth +$10M · each playoff win +$2.5M
  • Finals +$7.5M · Title +$15M

A full title run banks +$47.5M on top of the win money. There is no appearance money — win nothing and you earn nothing.

Spending

  • No salary cap and no luxury tax. What you earn is what you can spend
  • Minimum contract is $1M
  • Net revenue is the whole next-season budget — no carried cash

League-average budget is $100M — the number the salary curve is calibrated against, so an average team roughly breaks even.
