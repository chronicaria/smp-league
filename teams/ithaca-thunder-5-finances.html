How Finances Work

Revenue

  • Everyone banks $62M before a ball is thrown
  • Wins pay on a ladder: $2.2M for wins 1-9, then $1.6M for wins 10-18, then $1.1M for wins 19-27, then $0.6M for wins 28-36
  • Playoff berth +$4M · each playoff win +$1.5M · Finals +$3M · Title +$5M

A full title run banks +$21M on top of the ladder. Later wins pay less on purpose — running away with the league does not buy you a bigger war chest.

Spending

  • No salary cap and no luxury tax. What you earn is what you can spend
  • Minimum contract is $1M
  • Net revenue is the whole next-season budget — no carried cash

League-average budget is $100.7M — the number the salary curve is calibrated against, so an average team roughly breaks even.
